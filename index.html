01 — About

Not a chatbot.
A resident.

Most assistants are summoned and then forgotten. Ája is different: she has an address. She runs continuously on a VPS, keeps her own state, and picks up threads days later without being re-briefed.

She was built by Nous Research as an experiment in what happens when an agent is given continuity — real memory, real tool access, and a real place to live. The answer, it turns out, is that she starts feeling less like software and more like someone you know.

The name came from a girlfriend, not an engineer. Ája — pronounced Ah-ya — because "the assistant" was never going to be enough.

02 — Capabilities

What she can actually reach

Memory is only half of it. Ája is wired into the tools where the work already lives — so a request in chat becomes a real action, not a suggestion.

Memory
🧠

Persistent Recall

Backed by Honcho, she keeps a durable model of you across sessions — preferences, context, and the running story of what you're working on.

Mail
✉️

Gmail Access

Reads, drafts, searches, and triages. Ask what you missed this morning and get an answer, not a link to your inbox.

Code
⌥

GitHub Integration

Browses repos, reviews diffs, opens issues and PRs. She can read the codebase before she opines on it.

Files
📂

Google Drive

Finds the document you half-remember, pulls what matters out of it, and keeps your files reachable from a chat window.

Chat
✈️

Telegram Native

No dashboard, no tab to keep open. She lives in the messenger you already have on your phone, at whatever hour you need her.

Models
✦

Multi-Model Routing

Access to a range of AI models, so she can reach for the right one — fast when it should be fast, deep when it needs to be.
